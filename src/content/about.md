## Kit Care & Washing Guide

To keep your authentic jerseys in mint match condition for years to come:

* Always wash your shirt **inside out** to protect heat-applied numbers and crests.
* Wash in **cold water (30°C or below)** on a delicate cycle.
* **Never use fabric softener** — softeners break down the sweat-wicking properties of Dri-FIT ADV and HEAT.RDY fabrics.
* **Hang dry only in the shade.** Never put authentic football shirts in a heated tumble dryer.
* Do not iron directly over printed names, numbers, or sponsor logos.
