# Privacy Policy – Football Wears

Effective Date: 1 January 2025  
Last Updated: 11 February 2025  

## 1. Overview

At **Football Wears**, we value and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, handle, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, browse our football products, save items to your wishlist, and place orders.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

To fulfill your orders and provide a personalized football shopping experience, we may collect:

* **Contact Information:** Full name, email address, phone number, and delivery address.
* **Order History:** Details of kits, boots, sizes, and customizations purchased.
* **Local Storage Data:** We utilize your browser's local storage to save your active shopping cart items, wishlist preferences, and order history so your experience is fast and persistent.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to:

* Process and dispatch your football kit orders to your delivery address.
* Send package tracking numbers and delivery status notifications.
* Assist you with sizing inquiries, kit care advice, and customer support.
* Improve our product selection based on popular team kits and player requests.

---

## 4. Data Security & Third Parties

* We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers.
* Your shipping details are shared only with our trusted courier delivery partners (e.g., Speedaf, DHL) solely for the purpose of getting your package to your doorstep.
