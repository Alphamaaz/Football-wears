# Cookie & Storage Policy – Football Wears

Effective Date: 1 January 2025  

## 1. How Football Wears Uses Cookies and Local Storage

**Football Wears** uses cookies and browser local storage to provide a seamless and lightning-fast shopping experience without requiring cumbersome logins for basic browsing.

---

## 2. What We Store Locally

* **Shopping Cart State:** Remembers items, chosen sizes, and custom player printing you added to your cart as you browse between pages.
* **Wishlist Preferences:** Keeps your favorite saved jerseys and boots handy for quick access later.
* **Order History:** Saves references to your recent orders and shipment tracking details on your device.
* **Display Preferences:** Remembers whether you prefer browsing items in a 2-column grid or list view.

---

## 3. Managing Your Storage

You can clear your local storage and cookies at any time through your browser settings. Note that clearing storage will reset your active shopping cart and wishlist on that device.

For questions, contact **support@footballwears.shop**.
